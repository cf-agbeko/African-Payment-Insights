Making sense of Africa's changing payment rails

Decoding Africa's $1.4 Trillion Digital Rails.

Weekly deep-dives decoding mobile money, B2B merchant digitization, cross-border settlement systems, and regulatory shifts across Africa's 54 markets. Built for founders, investors, and operators.

Three pillars of focus

Infrastructure & Switches

We look past consumer apps to analyze the core rails: PAPSS, national instant payment switches, open banking APIs, and the real-time settlement networks changing how money moves.

Merchant & B2B Digitization

P2P transfers are mature. The real battle is offline. We dissect agent networks, informal retail supply chains, and B2B software solutions digitizing Africa's cash economy.

Cross-Border & Web3

Devaluing currencies and FX friction are driving a silent revolution. We monitor how stablecoins, liquidity marketplaces, and regional switches are building alternative trade pathways.
