Quick-Reference Fintech Glossary

The core terms behind Africa's payment rails, in plain language.

Account-to-Account (A2A)
Direct transfers between bank or mobile wallet accounts that bypass traditional card networks like Visa and Mastercard, drastically reducing merchant transaction fees.
Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
Society-wide open digital frameworks, such as national identity systems and unified, government-backed payment rails, designed to act as common utility rails.
Interoperability
The technological and regulatory ability of different financial systems — such as a commercial bank and an independent telecom mobile wallet — to seamlessly clear and settle transactions with one another.
PAPSS (Pan-African Payment and Settlement System)
A centralized financial switch developed by Afreximbank and the African Union, allowing African businesses to settle cross-border trade instantly using their local respective currencies.
USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data)
A lightweight GSM communication protocol that lets mobile banking users execute financial transactions via simple text menus on any phone — representing 63.5% of Africa's current mobile money transaction volume.
